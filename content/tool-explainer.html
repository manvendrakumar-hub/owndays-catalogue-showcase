Product explainer video

Built and used for 3 PIDs Many PIDs: planned, not built

A small pipeline of Python scripts turns one PID into two finished videos, English and Mandarin. It reads the product page, cuts the product photos out of their white background, measures where the lenses, the outline and the temple are, writes the lines from fixed templates, records them with ElevenLabs, and renders a square video where every on-screen line lands on the spoken word. Every product pixel is the real photo; nothing is generated.

Python scripts

ScriptWhat it doesNeeds
make_cutouts.pyTurns a white-background photo into a transparent cutout and removes the floor reflection. It has two modes: one for dark frames, and one for coloured or see-through frames and tinted lenses.OpenCV
measure.pyFinds the two lenses, the frame outline and the temple line on the cutouts, and checks that the lens width and the lens height give the same scale.OpenCV
make_project.pyFills the six scene templates with a PID's measured positions and its lines, and builds the English project.Nothing extra
build_vo.pyRecords each scene with ElevenLabs, reads the word timings and levels the audio.An ElevenLabs key
apply_timing.pyWrites the spoken-word times into the scenes and rebuilds the timeline.Nothing extra
localize.pyBuilds the Mandarin version from the English one, with its own timing and Chinese font.Google Fonts, for the Chinese font subset

What was done to make it

  1. CutoutsBackground out, product untouchedThe white ground is removed from the alpha, reflections are dropped and clear lenses stay clear. A second mode keeps coloured and see-through frames whole, keeps thin metal bridges, and removes the floor reflection column by column. Checked on dark and coloured backgrounds.
  2. ScenesSix scenes and a footerOutline, measurement lines and glint are drawn in photo coordinates, so they move and scale with the photo. Dimension lines are to scale. For a new PID the positions are measured from its cutouts. On the two new PIDs the lens width and height give the same scale to within 0.5%.
  3. TemplatesOne scene set for every PIDThe six scenes are templates. A new PID fills in its own positions and words, and the template is checked by rebuilding the first approved video from it.
  4. VoiceOne take per sceneThe voice's word timings decide when each line appears. Audio is levelled to about -15 LUFS with a peak ceiling.
  5. ScriptPlain statementsLines come from page facts only. Brand words are written so the voice says them as words.
  6. ChecksEvery build is checkedLint, layout and text contrast, contact sheets read at every spoken beat, and the sound checked for length and loudness.
  7. RenderAbout 3 minutes a PIDBoth languages on one Mac. Cost and time per PID are on the Cost and time tab.

Built with

  • RendererHyperFrames (HTML scenes to video) with ffmpeg.
  • VoiceElevenLabs, multilingual model: Christine for English, Lexi for Mandarin.
  • FontsFounders Grotesk; Noto Sans SC for Chinese.
  • Output1080 × 1080, 30 fps, H.264 video with AAC sound.
  • Proven onThree frames: 233791 (glossy black eyeglasses), 223715 (tortoise sunglasses with tinted lenses and a thin gold-coloured bridge) and 254954 (see-through brown eyeglasses from a character collaboration). Rimless and half-rim frames, which have no closed lens outline, are not tried yet. A new frame still needs its own wording and a look at the first render.
  • Not builtRunning many PIDs automatically. This is planned, not done.
