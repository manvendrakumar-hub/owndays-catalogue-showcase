Static images catalog

Built and used for 5 PIDs Many PIDs at once: planned, not built

A small web app, the Owndays Catalogue Static Studio, turns one PID into a size image. It reads the product page, downloads the photos, finds the front and the side photo by their shape, measures the frame, and fits it into the size template in two versions. The Brand Bouncer then checks both against the brand guidelines, and four pictures show where they differ (at the end of this page). The screen has three parts: the PID goes in on the left, the size template and the source data sit in the middle, and the brand gate is on the right.

What was done to make it

  1. FetchA PID becomes details and photosThe four sizes, the spec tiles and the gallery come from the product page. Where the size code printed on the frame is known, it is compared with the page and any difference is flagged.
  2. PickThe right two photosThe front photo has two matching lenses in the right proportions. The side photo is a long, thin shape. A person can override a pick, but the tool does not need one.
  3. MeasureArrow spots from the photosThe lens edges, the bridge and the temple are located in pixels. The millimetres on the call-outs are always the listed ones.
  4. FitTwo layouts from one measurementOne follows the Figma file. The other follows the guidelines: Semibold emphasis, the 10% safe area and the 12 × 12 grid.
  5. CheckThe Brand Bouncer15 automatic checks on the finished image, 4 items for the brand team and 2 that do not apply, each explained with the guideline's own words and its page.
  6. ShowEverything in one screenThe size template, the source data, the score for each version and the pictures of the differences. Download the image or save the PID.

Time and cost

About 15 seconds to fetch a PID from the site (short pauses are built in so the site is not hammered) and about 8 seconds to measure, fit both versions and run the checks, measured on the saved PIDs. No paid service is used, so the cash cost per PID is zero. Building the tool is not priced.

Built with

  • LanguagePython 3, with a plain web page. No framework and no extra service.
  • PhotosOpenCV and NumPy for finding and measuring the frame; Pillow for the pictures.
  • TemplateA single HTML page with Founders Grotesk, photographed to PNG with headless Chrome. Its geometry comes from the Figma Size Template.
  • Brand rulesOWNDAYS Brand Guidelines 1.0 (June 2024), 169 pages.
  • Output1080 × 1080 PNG, two versions per PID, plus four comparison pictures and a record of every check.
  • Proven onFive PIDs end to end, from glossy black acetate to tortoise-brown sunglasses and a translucent brown collaboration frame. The photo finder was tried on 71 PIDs from over 20 brands (68 found), and 12 of the hardest frames (tinted sunglasses, clear and pale acetate, wire) were fitted end to end and all came out right.
  • Not builtMany PIDs at once by brand or top-seller list, which waits on the lists from the business side. Rimless and half-rim frames, which have no closed lens outline. The other size-template layouts.
